About me

Hello! My name is Zander McFarland. I am an Interdisciplinary Studies Student at BYU-Idaho and an avid fan of theatre. I have been in many shows throughout my life and love being on stage. Some of my favorite roles have included Mike Talman in Wait Until Dark, Joseph in A Medieval Christmas Pageant: Plays from the York Cycle, and Adolfo Pirelli in Sweeney Todd. I see theatre as an important form of expression and creativity and my favorite form of storytelling. To me, it is also an opportunity to step into the shoes of divinity and create.

My Acting Philosophy:

My approach to acting is to get inside the mind of a character. The real crux of acting is in the subtext. What is this character thinking and feeling? What are their motivations? Why do they choose these words? If you can get into a character’s head, the acting takes care of itself.
